## What landed

State/history split on the new in-memory knowledge engine.

- Bindings carry `[validFrom, validTo)` intervals. Current is `validTo = null`. History is the interval list. There is no `canonicalStatus` and no `supersede` outcome.
- `reconcile()` is a pure slot state machine. Outcomes are exactly `re_assertion | change | correction | conflict | retraction | no_op`. It writes nothing.
- `update()` closes at most one interval and opens at most one interval per slot, and writes exactly one `StateTransition`. It writes no lifecycle field (`activationStatus`, `keepAlive`, `memoryStrength`, `canonicalStatus`, and the rest).
- CHANGE sequences a later instant (close old, open new). CORRECTION amends a past interval in place and retains a correction record. They do not collapse.
- CONFLICT marks competing stub claims `contested`, retains both, and leaves no accepted binding. Recency does not resolve it. UPDATE fails on a contested slot.
- RETRACTION closes the caused interval with recorded cause `retraction`; the interval is not deleted. Claim/event ids are `causedBy` strings. ACCEPT is not implemented.
