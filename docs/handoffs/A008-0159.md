## Delivered

[Working policy](../TASK_WORKFLOW.md#live-verification-budget) replaces blanket
paid-call escalation with inherited bounded authority for in-scope verification
on approved providers/credentials. Defaults: 10 SEK/task, 10 physical attempts,
16384 input and 4096 generated tokens per call, 120-second local deadline.
Workers, children, retries and resumed sessions share the same accounting.
The owner's subsequent in-scope clarification is explicit in policy, template
and platform §21.1: budget is a ceiling, not a consumption target. Stop when
sufficient verification is achieved; unused budget is desirable. No extra test,
model expense or scope is justified by budget availability alone.

Model-specific current price basis, input/output/reasoning and other charges,
currency conversion and conservative reservations govern spend. Free routes
retain non-monetary limits; unknown usage remains reserved. Budget increases,
new cost-bearing services and materially different effects require authority.

AGENTS, CONTRIBUTING, MULTIAGENT and the task template point to that policy.
Current status/map and the semantic extraction recipe no longer repeat blanket
escalation. The recipe's real 16384-token output cap remains documented and needs
a matching approved allocation; no script parameters changed.

[Platform v1.2](../A008_PLATFORM_SPEC.md) includes the owner's replacement §27,
§21.1 and A37-A40 future budget/verification scenarios. Separate execution
verification and semantic ownership remain intact.
