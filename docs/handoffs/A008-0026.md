## What landed

First-class evidence records, named ACCEPT, and the §9.3 sectioned payload.

- `Utterance` is a speech act with speaker, act, contentKind, content, clocks.
- `Claim` is born `asserted`. `ACCEPT` is the only process that may change `Claim.status`.
- `Provenance` is a relation (`appears_in`, `derived_from`), not a bag of fields.
- `INGEST` writes artifact + raw utterance only. It writes no claims, bindings, state, or lifecycle.
- Recitation, quotation, hypothetical, and question never produce world claims. The prayer "Fader vår som är i himmelen" is recognized as `recitation` / `prayer`.
- A prediction may produce a future-interval claim and is rejected if asked to open a current-state binding (`validTo = null`).
- Identified policy `user-assertion-v1` may accept a verified user assertion. It does not write `keepAlive`, strength, activation, or bindings. Analyzer confidence cannot accept. Third-party speakers remain `asserted`.
- Payload composer emits `{ scope, state, history, events, utterances, claims, artifacts, provenance }`. Runtime/knowledge ids, lifecycle numbers, retrieval scores, and audit never appear. History always carries an interval; claims always carry attribution and status; utterances always carry speaker and speech act. Evidence-only composition leaves `state` empty (bindings are M4).
