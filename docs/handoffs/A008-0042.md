## What the siblings must know

The contract they consume is exported from `src/ingest/index.js`:

- `SourceExtractorRegistry.extract(input)` returns `ExtractedSource`
  (`content`, `speaker`, `relation`, optional `contentKind`) or throws
  `SourceIngestError`.
- `sniffSourceMediaType(bytes)` decides the media type. A declared filename or
  extension must never be passed to it or used instead of it.
- `SourceIngestError.code` is `unsupported_media_type`, `invalid_source`, or
  `description_failed`, and `mediaType` names the offending type. A008-0044
  should surface `code` and `mediaType` to the renderer, not a generic failure.
- A registry is composed, not global. A008-0043 builds one from
  `Utf8TextExtractor` plus, when a vision model is configured,
  `DescribedImageExtractor(new NvidiaImageDescriber(...))`.

## Risks

1. **The ZIP family reports as DOCX.** An XLSX or PPTX upload will be refused
   with a message naming the DOCX type. Truthful about the family, imprecise
   about the member; distinguishing them needs a ZIP reader.
2. **`isProbablyUtf8Text` re-reads the whole input** when the 8 KiB window fails,
   so a large binary that is invalid only late costs one extra full decode. The
   byte cap in A008-0044 bounds that.
3. **No vision model exists in the registry.** `DescribedImageExtractor` is
   ready, but until a model is added and cost authority granted, image uploads
   will reach the unsupported error unless A008-0043 composes a describer.
