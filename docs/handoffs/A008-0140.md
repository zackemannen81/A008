## Restart semantics

- `serverInstanceId` is the process epoch visible to clients.
- Receipt and resume state are deliberately process-local. Restart destroys them.
- A client that loses a process-local command outcome must surface uncertainty; it must not infer failure/success or automatically retry the mutation.
- Old resume authority cannot resurrect a dead-process session.
- This remains intentionally weaker than durable sessions or durable exactly-once execution.
